Ten years ago, a small group of developers made a pivotal decision. They started with a simple "fork" in code and a radical vision: **every church—regardless of its size, location, or budget—deserves to own its data.**

In an era where data is often harvested and sold, ChurchCRM was built on the foundation of **data sovereignty.** We believed then, as we do now, that ministry tools should empower the church, not exploit it.

### From a Vision to a Global Collective

Fast forward to today, and that vision has blossomed into a global reality. ChurchCRM is no longer just a project; it's a steadfast companion for congregations around the world.

* **49 Languages:** Our platform is accessible to ministries across the globe.
* **100% Volunteer-Led:** We aren't a corporation with a bottom line. We are a community driven by a shared mission.
* **Open Source Roots:** We provide professional-grade tools for free, ensuring that financial barriers never stand in the way of effective ministry.

### Building the Next Decade — We Need You!

As we step into our second decade, the landscape of digital ministry is evolving. To keep pace with the needs of modern churches and the rollout of our **new V7.0 plugin system**, we are looking for passionate people to join the movement.

Whether you have **two hours a month or two hours a day**, your skills can help a pastor manage their congregation more effectively or help a mission protect their members' privacy.

### Why Join the ChurchCRM Movement?

Contributing to open source is about more than just writing code or translating text. It's about:

1. **High-Impact Service:** Work on a real-world project used by thousands of churches daily.
2. **Data Privacy:** Help churches maintain absolute control over their sensitive information.
3. **Community:** Join a global collective of faith-driven engineers, creators, and leaders.
