# ChurchCRM Visual Style & UI Theme

## 🎨 Brand Palette (The "Common Theme")
* **Primary:** Deep Sabbath Teal (`#1B4D4E`) - Represents depth, peace, and growth.
* **Accent:** Warm Earth Clay (`#D98E73`) - Represents humanity, warmth, and action.
* **Background:** Soft Linen (`#F9F8F6`) - Replaces harsh whites for a "breathable" feel.
* **Text:** Charcoal Slate (`#2D2D2D`) - High readability for all ages.

## 📐 UI/UX Guidelines (Soft-Tech Minimalism)
* **Corner Radius:** All buttons and cards should have a `12px` rounded corner. No sharp edges.
* **Typography:** * *Headings:* A warm Serif (e.g., Playfair Display) for a "classic/trusted" feel.
    * *Body:* A clean, high-legibility Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Montserrat).
* **Iconography:** Thin-line art, monochromatic (Teal). Avoid heavy filled icons.

## 📸 Imagery Direction
* **Style:** Candid, natural lighting, high depth-of-field.
* **Subject:** People in motion—shaking hands, drinking coffee, laughing in small groups. 
* **Rule:** Screens should be secondary in photos. The *result* of the software is the focus.
