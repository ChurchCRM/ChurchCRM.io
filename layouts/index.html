Help your church get organized

ChurchCRM helps you manage the things your church already does: track people and families, organize groups and ministries, coordinate events, give and receive gifts, and empower volunteers.

Know your congregation

Member profiles, family relationships, contact information, and notes — all in one searchable place.

Organize your groups

Groups, ministries, classes — manage membership, roles, and communications in one place.

Track events and attendance

Services, programs, events — record attendance, build historical records, and view trends.

Track giving and finances

Record donations, generate tax receipts, run financial reports, and understand your giving trends.

Built for churches that value simplicity and independence

Free. No subscription.

ChurchCRM is open source and genuinely free to use. You're not locked into a monthly bill or a vendor. Download it, install it, use it.

You own your data.

Your church data belongs to your church. Export it anytime. Move it. Back it up. You're not dependent on ChurchCRM staying around or remaining its provider forever.

Open source means transparency.

The code is open for anyone to inspect. No hidden tracking, no surprises. The community helps build it. Security is everyone's responsibility.

Made by churches, for churches.

ChurchCRM has been actively maintained and improved for over a decade, based on real feedback from real churches using it every day.

You're not on your own

Documentation, community support, and a welcoming Discord community are here to help.
