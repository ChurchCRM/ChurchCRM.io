Why ChurchCRM exists

ChurchCRM was created as a service to the Church — not as a product to sell to it.

It began because churches needed a way to keep track of the people they serve, and the tools available asked them to pay monthly for the privilege of storing their own congregation's information.

Today it is maintained in the open by developers and church staff who use it themselves. Every change is public. Every install belongs to the church running it.

01 — People

Start with your people.

People and families are the foundation of everything else. Records, relationships, roles and history live together, so the rest of the system has something real to stand on.

Read the people & families docs

02 — The work

Organize the work of the church.

Groups, events, attendance and communication — the things a church actually does week to week, handled without spreadsheets in between.

  • GroupsRoles, membership, assignment
  • EventsCalendar and check-in
  • AttendanceRecorded over time
  • CommunicationEmail and letters

03 — Giving

Keep track of giving and finances.

Pledges, deposits, funds and statements — enough structure to be accountable to your congregation, without turning the office into an accounting department.
