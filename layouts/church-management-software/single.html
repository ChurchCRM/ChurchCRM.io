What churches accomplish with ChurchCRM

No feature dump. Here are the real jobs ChurchCRM helps churches do.

  • Manage your people and familiesKeep a single, searchable directory of members and families. Track contact info, notes, pastoral care, and photos. Search by name, address, or phone number.
  • Organize groups and ministriesTrack small groups, bible studies, committees, volunteers, and ministry teams. Manage group membership and roles, and send group email from the same place.
  • Plan events and track attendanceCreate and manage events, track attendance for services and programs, and use self-service kiosks for check-in. Works for Sunday School, Bible studies, youth groups, and more.
  • Track giving and financesRecord donations, track giving patterns, run financial reports, and generate giving reports for tax statements. No external accounting required — ChurchCRM handles what churches need.
  • Volunteers and member onboardingSelf-service registration and member verification. Track volunteer opportunities members have signed up for. Reduce manual data entry for new member paperwork.
  • Reports and insightsGenerate attendance trends, giving analysis, and membership reports. Export data for deeper analysis or archive. Full control — your data stays in your hands.

Is ChurchCRM right for your church?

ChurchCRM works best for churches that:

  • Need practical church management — member directory, groups, attendance, giving, volunteers.
  • Want to own their data — no vendor lock-in, no subscription pressure.
  • Prefer self-hosting or simple hosting — can deploy to affordable shared hosting or their own server.
  • Work in multiple languages — benefit from community translations in 49 languages.

ChurchCRM may not be the fit if: Your church needs integrated email campaigns, paid mobile apps, vendor phone support, or turnkey event ticketing. That's okay — those are legitimate needs, and there are products designed for them. We'll help you understand your options.

Understanding self-hosting

The software is free. Hosting costs depend on what you have available.

ChurchCRM runs on a standard web server (PHP + MySQL). Your costs depend on how you host:

  • Existing server or donated hosting: $0/month. If your church already has a server or someone has donated hosting, you may have no additional cost.
  • Shared hosting: $3–10/month. Easy setup. Works for most churches. No technical skills required beyond following installation guides.
  • Docker / VPS: $5–50/month depending on traffic. ChurchCRM is developed and tested with Docker, but there is no officially maintained production image — your IT volunteer is responsible for keeping the container patched and secure. Full control, more responsibility.

Pro tip: Email delivery may cost extra ($10–20/month if you send many emails). This is optional and separate from ChurchCRM.

Common questions

Can we migrate from another system?

Yes. ChurchCRM supports CSV import for member and family data, plus database restore for a full migration. Setup time depends on how much cleanup your existing data needs.

What if we need customization?

Plugins and integrations exist. Custom plugins require developer skills but source code is open — you can hire someone to build what you need.

Can we use it offline?

No. ChurchCRM is a web application. Your IT person can run it on a local server if you have no internet, but that's advanced.

What about security and backups?

Built-in roles and permissions. Backups depend on your hosting — shared hosts usually include them; DIY hosting is your responsibility.

More questions about cost, technical staff, or getting help? See the full FAQ. For technical documentation, see docs.churchcrm.io or ask in the Discord community.
